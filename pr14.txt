Aim: To study different types of blockchain technology and understand their real-world applications.

## 1. Introduction

Blockchain is a distributed digital ledger that stores information in blocks connected using cryptography. The data is shared across a network of computers, making it transparent, secure and difficult to modify.

Blockchain technology is used in cryptocurrency, banking, healthcare, supply chain management, digital identity and many other industries.

## 5. Advantages of blockchain

1. Transparency: Transactions can be verified by authorized participants.
2. Security: Cryptography helps protect transaction records.
3. Decentralization: Public blockchain networks do not depend on a single central authority.
4. Traceability: Transactions and asset movements can be tracked.
5. Immutability: Recorded data is difficult to modify without detection.
6. Automation: Smart contracts can automate business processes.

## 6. Limitations of blockchain

1. Some blockchain networks have limited transaction speed.
2. Transaction fees can increase during high network demand.
3. Private information must be handled carefully.
4. Blockchain implementation can be expensive and complex.
5. Lost private keys can result in loss of access to digital assets.
6. Regulatory requirements may differ across countries.

## 7. Conclusion

Blockchain is an emerging technology that provides secure, transparent and distributed data management. There are four major types of blockchain: Public, Private, Consortium and Hybrid. Each type has different features and applications.

Blockchain is useful in banking, healthcare, supply chain management, digital identity, real estate and many other industries. Selecting the appropriate blockchain depends on the organization's requirements for security, privacy, transparency and access control.

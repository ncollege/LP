## Understanding Ether transaction fees

The transaction fee is calculated as:

\\[ \text{Transaction Fee}=\text{Gas Used}\times\text{Effective Gas Price} \\]

For example:

- Gas used = 21,000
- Gas price = 10 Gwei

\\[ 21000\times10=210000\text{ Gwei} \\]

\\[ =0.00021\text{ ETH} \\]

This is an illustrative example, not an actual transaction fee.

1. Open your MetaMask wallet.
2. Click on your account name or address.
3. Copy your wallet address.
4. This public address is used to receive cryptocurrency.

### Step 5: Understand wallet features

Explore the following options in MetaMask:

- Account: Displays your wallet account.
- Receive: Shows your wallet address for receiving crypto.
- Send: Used to send cryptocurrency.
- Activity: Displays transaction history.
- Network: Allows you to select a blockchain network.

### Step 6: Test a crypto transaction

1. Select the Ethereum Sepolia test network.
2. Get test ETH from a Sepolia faucet.
3. Open the Send option.
4. Enter another test wallet address.
5. Enter a small test amount.
6. Review the gas fee.
7. Click Confirm.

### Step 7: Check transaction history

1. Open the Activity section.
2. Select the completed transaction.
3. Click View on block explorer.
4. Check transaction status, amount, wallet addresses and gas fee.

[Sepolia Block Explorer](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
